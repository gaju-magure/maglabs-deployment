Category-by-category playbook that shows exactly what happens at each stage of the pipeline for every ideation category in your taxonomy.

For each category you’ll see:
	1.	Capture – extra fields & automations in the submission form
	2.	Screen – AI or rules-based checks that run automatically
	3.	Evaluate – weighted criteria & the board that scores the idea
	4.	Approve – thresholds / governance gate
	5.	Incubate – typical delivery track, KPIs & hand-off

Baseline logic (shared by all categories)
	•	Idea saved → AI multi-label tagging → automated Screen → fork to the right board → converge on PMO / Incubation once approved.

▲ Product Innovation

Step	Specialisation
Capture	Fields : problem statement, target persona, value proposition, feature differentiation.
Screen	Similarity check against roadmap epics; ROI calc from price × TAM.
Evaluate	Weights : Market 30 % · Strategic Fit 25 % · Feasibility 20 % · ROI 15 % · Risk 10 %. Product SteerCo votes.
Approve	Composite ≥ 75 % and roadmap slot available next two quarters.
Incubate	Auto-create Jira Epic → Scrum team; track velocity, feature adoption, NPS delta.

▲ Service Innovation

Step	Specialisation
Capture	SLA targets, delivery model (onsite, SaaS, managed), required capabilities.
Screen	Capacity simulation vs current workforce; benchmark margin model.
Evaluate	CX Lift 25 % · Gross Margin 25 % · Feasibility 20 % · Strategic Fit 20 % · Risk 10 %.
Approve	Margin ≥ 30 %, CX lift forecast ≥ +5 NPS.
Incubate	Service Design sprint → pilot customer; measure NPS, attach-rate, churn.
