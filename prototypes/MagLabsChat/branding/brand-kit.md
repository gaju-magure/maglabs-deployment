# Magure.ai Brand Guidelines

---

## Logo Usage Guidelines

- **Clear Space:** Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "M" symbol.
- **Minimum Size:**
  - Digital use: Minimum height of 24px.
  - Recommended heights: 32px, 48px for best visibility.
- **Alterations:** Do not distort, recolor, or apply effects to the logo.
- **Backgrounds:** Preferably use on dark or neutral backgrounds for best visibility.
- **Variations:**
  - Gradient color logo.
  - Neutral color (black/white) versions available.
- **Avoid:** Adding drop shadows or using unapproved colors or orientations.

- **Gradient Start:** `#FDA052` (Orange)

### Color Combination Guidelines
- Employ gradients to represent creativity, trust, and technological innovation.
- Use solid colors sparingly and intentionally for emphasis or interaction highlights.
- Ensure sufficient contrast between foreground (text/icons) and background for clarity.

## Typography

- **Primary Font:** Satoshi (Note: This document uses Roboto/Helvetica as a fallback. For web and print, ensure Satoshi font files are properly licensed and embedded.)
- **Font Usage:**
  - Headlines: **Bold, UPPERCASE**.
  - Body Text: Medium weight, clean, readable.
  - Accent: *Light italic* for emphasis.
- **Web-safe alternative:**
  - Use Montserrat or Roboto as fallback fonts if Satoshi is unavailable.

---

## Imagery Style

- **Preferred Style:** Futuristic, tech-focused, AI-themed, vibrant, and engaging visuals.
- **Photography:**
  - Balance between realistic (e.g., human interactions with tech) and abstract representations of data and connectivity.
- **Image Treatments:**
  - Utilize gradient overlays or subtle tech textures where appropriate.
  - Maintain clean borders; avoid heavy or distracting frames.
- **Icons & Graphics:**
  - Style preference: modern, flat, filled or outlined icons.
  - Maintain uniform stroke widths for consistency.

---

## Layout and Spacing

- **Grid System:** Use an 8px or 12px baseline grid for web/app layouts.
- **Margins & Padding:**
  - Standard outer margins: 24px–32px.
  - Standard padding: 8px, 16px, 24px increments.
- **Spacing & Alignment:**
  - Elements should consistently align vertically and horizontally.
  - Text alignment: left-aligned or centered based on context.

---

## Iconography

- **Style:** Modern, minimalistic, flat line or solid icons.
- **Color Usage:** Primarily utilize brand colors or neutral shades (white, black).
- **Size & Placement:** Consistent dimensions of 24px, 32px, or 48px depending on usage context.

---

## Patterns and Textures

- **Approved Patterns:**
  - Geometric shapes and tech-inspired abstract motifs.
  - Gradients blending primary brand colors.
- **Usage Rules:**
  - Suitable for backgrounds, hero sections, or decorative accents.
  - Maintain subtlety; patterns must not overpower primary content.

---

## Button and Component Styles

- **Buttons:**
  - Rounded corners (6–12px radius recommended).
  - Gradient or solid primary brand colors.
  - Clear hover, active, and disabled states differentiated by opacity and subtle shadow effects.
- **Links:**
  - Underline or gradient color hover state.
  - Maintain accessible contrast levels.

---

## Do's and Don'ts

### ✅ Do:
- Consistently use the defined gradient palette for visual elements.
- Keep logos clear and legible.
- Ensure readable typography, utilizing primary brand fonts.
- Maintain consistent spacing and alignment rules.

### ❌ Don't:
- Distort or alter the logo shape or colors.
- Add shadows, outlines, or unauthorized colors to logos or icons.
- Mix too many brand colors simultaneously.
- Use excessively heavy filters or distracting visual effects on images.
