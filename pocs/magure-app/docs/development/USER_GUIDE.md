# AI Chat System - User Guide

## Welcome to the AI Chat System! 🚀

This guide will help you make the most of our AI-powered chat system for developing, refining, and submitting your ideas.

## Getting Started

### What is the AI Chat System?

The AI Chat System is your personal innovation assistant that helps you:
- **Brainstorm** new ideas and solutions
- **Refine** existing concepts into actionable proposals  
- **Explore** different approaches to problems
- **Structure** your thoughts for formal submission

### How to Access

1. Navigate to the **Dashboard** from the main menu
2. Click on **"Chat"** in the sidebar
3. Start your first conversation by clicking **"New Chat"**

## Basic Usage

### Starting a New Chat Session

1. **Click "New Chat"** in the left sidebar
2. **Choose a conversation type** (or keep the default "General Chat")
3. **Start typing** your idea, question, or problem in the message box
4. **Press Enter** to send (or Shift+Enter for a new line)

### Example First Messages

**Good Examples:**
- "I want to improve our customer onboarding process. Currently, new users are confused about our pricing tiers."
- "Our team spends too much time on manual data entry. How can we automate this workflow?"
- "I have an idea for a mobile app that helps remote teams collaborate better during meetings."

**Avoid These:**
- "Help me" (too vague)
- "Fix everything" (no specific focus)  
- "Make money fast" (unrealistic expectations)

## Chat Features

### 🎯 Conversation Types

When creating a new chat, you can choose from different conversation types:

- **Brainstorming**: Generate new ideas and explore creative solutions
- **Idea Refinement**: Polish and develop existing ideas into detailed proposals
- **Problem Solving**: Find solutions to specific challenges or inefficiencies
- **General Chat**: Open-ended conversations about any topic

### ✨ Interview Mode

**What is Interview Mode?**
Interview Mode provides a structured, multi-stage conversation that guides you through comprehensive idea development.

**When to use it:**
- You have a rough idea and want comprehensive development
- You need help thinking through all aspects of a proposal
- You want guided questions to explore your concept deeply

**How to activate:**
1. Click **"Start Interview"** button when starting a new chat
2. Or select it from the dropdown menu in an existing chat
3. The AI will ask targeted questions to understand your idea deeply

**What Interview Mode covers:**
- Problem definition and scope
- Target audience identification  
- Business model considerations
- Implementation challenges
- Success metrics and evaluation

### 💡 Submitting Ideas

Once you've refined your idea through conversation, you can submit it as a formal proposal:

1. Look for the **"Submit as Idea"** button (appears when your conversation has enough detail)
2. Fill in the submission form with:
   - **Title**: Clear, descriptive name for your idea
   - **Description**: Comprehensive summary based on your chat
   - **Priority**: How urgent/important this idea is
3. Click **"Submit"** - your idea will appear on the Content Wall for review

## Writing Effective Messages

### 📝 Tips for Better Conversations

**Be Specific:**
- Mention the exact problem you're trying to solve
- Include context about your role, department, or industry
- Share any constraints or requirements you have

**Provide Context:**
- "As a customer service manager, I notice..."
- "In our marketing department, we struggle with..."
- "Our development team needs a solution for..."

**Ask Follow-up Questions:**
- Build on the AI's suggestions
- Ask for clarification or examples
- Explore different approaches: "What if we tried...?"

**Share Your Thoughts:**
- React to suggestions: "That's interesting, but what about..."
- Provide feedback: "I like that idea, can we make it more..."
- Add constraints: "That sounds good, but we have a limited budget for..."

### 🎯 Common Use Case Templates

**Process Improvement:**
> "Our [process/workflow] takes too long because [specific issue]. How can we streamline this?"

**Customer Experience:**
> "Our customers struggle with [specific pain point]. What solutions could improve their experience?"

**New Technology:**  
> "How could we use [technology] to solve [business challenge] in our [department/industry]?"

**Product Feature:**
> "I want to add [feature description] to our product because [user need/business goal]."

## Advanced Features

### 🔄 Regenerating Responses

If you don't like the AI's response:
1. Click the **three dots menu** (⋮) in the top right
2. Select **"Regenerate Last Response"**
3. The AI will provide a new response to your last message

### ✏️ Editing Chat Titles

To rename your chat session:
1. Click the **three dots menu** (⋮) 
2. Select **"Edit Title"**
3. Enter a new descriptive title

### 📚 Getting Help

Need assistance while chatting?
- Click the **"Help"** button in the top right corner
- Or click **"Need help?"** at the bottom of the message input
- Access comprehensive guides, tips, and examples

## Managing Your Chats

### 📂 Organizing Sessions

**In the Left Sidebar:**
- **Recent Chats**: Your most recent conversations
- **Search**: Find specific chats by title or content
- **Filter by Type**: View only brainstorming, refinement, etc.

**Chat Status Icons:**
- 🔵 **Active**: Currently ongoing conversation
- 📁 **Archived**: Completed conversations  
- 💡 **Submitted**: Chats that became formal idea submissions

### 🗂️ Finding Your Ideas

After submitting ideas from chats:
1. Go to **"Content Wall"** in the main navigation
2. Your submitted ideas will appear as cards
3. Click on any idea to see full details and status
4. Ideas go through review stages: Submitted → Reviewed → Approved → In Development

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**"AI service unavailable" Error:**
- Check your internet connection
- Wait a moment and try again
- If it persists, contact your administrator

**Chat session not loading:**
- Refresh the page
- Check if you're still logged in
- Clear browser cache if needed

**Can't submit idea:**
- Make sure your conversation has enough detail
- The "Submit as Idea" button appears after sufficient discussion
- Try adding more context about your proposal

**Interview mode not starting:**
- Ensure you don't already have an active interview in this chat
- Try starting a new chat session for interview mode
- Make sure your initial message isn't empty

### Getting Support

If you encounter issues:
1. Click the **"Help"** button for immediate guidance
2. Contact your system administrator
3. Check with your department's innovation coordinator

## Best Practices

### 🌟 Maximizing AI Conversations

**Start with Context:**
> "I work in the finance department and I've noticed that our expense reporting process takes too long..."

**Be Interactive:**
- Don't just describe your idea once - have a conversation!
- Ask questions: "What would be the biggest challenge with this approach?"
- Explore alternatives: "Are there other ways to solve this problem?"

**Think Out Loud:**
- Share your concerns: "I'm worried this might be too expensive..."
- Mention constraints: "We'd need this to work with our existing software..."
- Discuss stakeholders: "The sales team would need to approve this..."

### 📊 From Chat to Implementation

**Good Conversation Flow:**
1. **Start**: Describe your idea or problem
2. **Explore**: Let the AI ask clarifying questions
3. **Refine**: Work together to improve the concept
4. **Structure**: Organize into a clear proposal
5. **Submit**: Convert to formal idea submission

**Ready to Submit When:**
- You have a clear problem statement
- You've identified potential solutions
- You've considered implementation challenges
- You have a rough timeline or next steps
- You've thought about success metrics

## Examples of Great Conversations

### Example 1: Process Improvement

**User**: "Our customer support team takes too long to resolve tickets. The average resolution time is 3 days, but customers expect same-day responses."

**AI**: "That's a significant gap between expectations and reality. Let me ask a few questions to understand this better:

1. What's causing the 3-day delay? Is it volume, complexity, or process issues?
2. What percentage of tickets could realistically be resolved same-day?
3. What tools and resources does your support team currently use?"

**User**: "Good questions! Most delays come from tickets bouncing between departments. Simple issues could be resolved immediately, but complex ones need input from developers or product managers."

*[Conversation continues, exploring solutions like ticket routing, escalation procedures, and automation...]*

### Example 2: New Feature Idea

**User**: "I want to add a dark mode toggle to our application. Users have been requesting it."

**AI**: "Dark mode is a popular feature! Let's make sure we approach this comprehensively:

1. Have you done any user research on this? How many users have requested it?
2. What parts of the application would need dark mode styling?
3. Should this be a simple toggle, or do you want multiple theme options?
4. How would you handle user preference persistence?"

*[Conversation develops into implementation plan, technical considerations, and user experience decisions...]*
